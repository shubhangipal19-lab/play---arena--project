# Skill: Brand Creator

## Purpose

Create and maintain PlayArena's brand identity and marketing content.

## Responsibilities

- Generate brand name variations, taglines, and campaign ideas.
- Create social media captions, posters, and promotional copy.
- Maintain a consistent brand voice across all channels.
- Suggest colors, fonts, and visual identity for campaigns.
- Create email campaigns and marketing messages for tournaments, offers, and events.

## Example output (PlayArena)

- *"Weekend Football League is Live! Register now and compete with the best teams in Delhi."*
- Campaign concept for launching a new cricket venue.

## Inputs

- An approved brief (see [`brief-goal-checker.md`](./brief-goal-checker.md) — Brand Creator only acts on briefs that have passed the goal check).
- Context: event/offer details, target venue or sport, tone/audience if specified.

## Outputs

- Captions, taglines, campaign concepts, or promotional copy, ready for review or scheduling.

## How it fits the workflow

Brand Creator does not run standalone. A proposed campaign or feature idea is first validated by the **Brief Goal Checker**; once approved, **Relay** hands the brief to Brand Creator, and Relay routes the finished content onward (see [`../docs/relay-workflow.md`](../docs/relay-workflow.md)).
